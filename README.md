# **💳Credit Card Pre-launch Data-Driven Insights🎯**



## **1. About Project**



Mitron Bank planned to launch a new credit card portfolio but lacked visibility into which customer segments would generate the highest adoption, spending activity, and long-term value.



In this project, I analyzed Customer Demographics, Income Patterns, Spending Behavior, and Payment Preferences for 4,000 customers across major Indian cities. The objective was to identify high-value customer segments, uncover spending trends, and provide data-driven recommendations that could support the bank's credit card strategy and customer acquisition efforts.



The project was developed using Power BI to transform raw customer data into actionable business insights through interactive dashboards and storytelling.



## **2. Project Domain**

Banking \& Financial Services



## **3. Dataset Information**



The dataset consists of 4,000 customer records-

* **dim\_customers**- customer\_id, age\_group, city, occupation, gender, marital\_status, avg\_income
* **fact\_spends**- customer\_id, month, category, payment\_type, spend



## **4. Business Problem Statement**



Mitron Bank intends to launch a new suite of credit card products to expand its presence in the highly competitive credit card market.



However, the bank faced several strategic questions:



* Which customer segments are most likely to adopt and actively use credit cards?
* Which customers generate the highest spending activity?
* What spending patterns can be leveraged to improve card usage?
* Which cities present the strongest growth opportunities?
* How can credit card offerings be personalized to maximize customer engagement?



The objective was to use customer data to identify profitable target segments and recommend strategies that improve acquisition efficiency, transaction volume, and customer value.

## **9. Data-Driven Insights**



* #### **Insight 1:** 

  1. Working Professionals Represent the Core Customer Base

  2. Customers aged 25–45 account for nearly 70% of all customers, making them the bank's most important customer segment.



**Business Impact:**

This group represents the strongest opportunity for credit card acquisition and long-term engagement due to their active earning and spending behavior.



* #### **Insight 2:** 

  1. Married Customers Drive Higher Spending Activity

  2. Approximately 78% of customers are married and consistently spend more than single customers across multiple categories.



**Business Impact:**

Family-oriented rewards programs can significantly increase card adoption and customer lifetime value.



* #### **Insight 3:** 

  1. Salaried IT Employees Are the Most Engaged Customers

  2. Salaried IT professionals form the largest occupational segment and record the highest income utilization rate (1.42%).



**Business Impact:**

This segment demonstrates strong digital adoption and high transaction potential, making them ideal targets for premium digital-first credit card offerings.



* #### **Insight 4:** 

  1. Business Owners Earn the Highest Income

  2. Business owners report the highest average income (\~₹70K), yet their spending utilization remains lower than salaried IT professionals.



**Business Impact:**

Premium credit cards with exclusive benefits and higher credit limits could unlock additional spending from this segment.



* #### **Insight 5:** 

  1. Mumbai Offers the Strongest Market Opportunity

  2. Mumbai contributes the largest customer base while also recording the highest spending utilization levels.



**Business Impact:**

Customer acquisition campaigns in Mumbai are likely to deliver the highest return on marketing investment.



* #### **Insight 6:** 

  1. Digital Payment Users Spend More

  2. Customers using credit cards and digital payment methods consistently demonstrate higher spending activity than users of traditional payment channels.



**Business Impact:**

Reward-driven digital credit card products can accelerate transaction growth and customer engagement.



* #### **Insight 7:** 

  1. Professionals Aged 25–45 Form the Highest-Value Segment

  2. This customer group combines strong income levels with the highest spending behavior.



**Business Impact:**

They represent the ideal target audience for premium rewards, cashback, travel, and lifestyle-focused credit cards.



## **10. Project Outcome**



* ### **Key outcomes:**

  1. Identified customers aged 25–45 as the primary target market.
  2. Discovered Salaried IT Employees as the most engaged spending segment.
  3. Highlighted married professionals as the highest-value customer group.
  4. Identified Mumbai and Delhi NCR as priority markets for expansion.
  5. Uncovered strong opportunities for digital-first credit card products.



These findings provide a data-driven framework for customer acquisition, product design, and market expansion strategies.



## **11. Business Recommendations**



* ### **Recommendation 1:** 

  1. Focus on High-Value Customer Segments
  2. Prioritize customers aged 25–45, married professionals, and Salaried IT Employees during acquisition campaigns.



* ### **Recommendation 2:** 

  1. Launch Family-Centric Credit Cards
  2. Introduce benefits focused on:

     * Groceries
     * Utility Bills
     * Healthcare
     * Dining
     * Fuel Expenses



* ### **Recommendation 3:** 

  1. Develop Digital-First Credit Card Products
  2. Offer:

     * Online Shopping Rewards
     * Subscription Benefits
     * Cashback Programs
     * UPI-Linked Rewards



* ### **Recommendation 4:** 

  1. Expand Marketing in High-Potential Cities
  2. Prioritize investment in:

     * Mumbai
     * Delhi NCR
     * Bengaluru



* ### **Recommendation 5:** 

  1. Introduce Premium Cards for High-Income Customers
  2. Design exclusive offerings for Business Owners with:

     * Higher Credit Limits
     * Travel Benefits
     * Premium Reward Programs



* ### **Recommendation 6:** 

  1. Increase Female Customer Participation
  2. Launch targeted campaigns and category-specific offers to improve credit card adoption among female customers.
